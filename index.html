Rajdhani Carpets

Carpet manufacturing · roll-based production

PehleMultiple WhatsApp groups
AbMobile app ERP for the whole floor
The big pain points
  • Orders, production and dispatch updates lived in separate WhatsApp groups. Ek order dhoondhna matlab sau messages scroll karna.
  • No record of which carpet roll was where. Rolls were found by asking people.
  • Wastage never measured, so true cost per order was a guess.
What changed
  • The whole team works on a phone app, as easy to use as WhatsApp.
  • Every roll has a QR. Scan it and see its order, stage and location.
  • Owner sees orders, stock and production live, without calling anyone.
Read the full case study

How it was before. Rajdhani ran day-to-day operations on WhatsApp: one group for orders, one for production, one for dispatch. It worked when the business was small. As volume grew, finding the status of a single order meant scrolling, forwarding and calling.

  • Owner could not see live status without calling someone.
  • When a mistake happened, there was no way to see who entered what.

What we built. A mobile-first ERP designed for the factory floor, not just the office.

  • Orders with their full journey, from booking to dispatch.
  • Roll-level QR tracking across the warehouse.
  • Recipe and batch records with planned vs actual material, so wastage becomes a number.
  • Stock alerts, purchase approvals and notifications on phone.
  • User permissions and an activity log for every action.

PehleHundreds of Excel sheets
AbOne custom ERP + CRM

The big pain points
  • Order book, raw material, packaging and purchase in separate sheets. Artwork status only in people's heads.
  • Batch size calculated by hand, every time. 3,000 pcs of a 100 ml product had to be turned into 300 kg manually.

Read the full case study

How it was before. Dermat makes finished, branded cosmetics for other brands: face washes, creams, shampoos. Every client gets a custom formulation, so there is no fixed product list. A dozen people worked off the same Excel order book every day.

  • No wastage tracking. If 1,000 were made and 900 shipped, nobody could see it.
  • A sales tool bought earlier was never adopted by the team.

What we built. A custom ERP + CRM designed around their order lifecycle, with a dense table view that feels like the Excel their team already knew.

  • Automatic batch-size calculation and formula (BOM) explosion.
  • Product code automatically creates its carton and label items, ready for a PO.
  • One-click quotation request to multiple vendors.
  • Machine, operator and shift mapping. Purchase reports for any period.
  • Tally sync, GST and e-way bill. Unlimited users, on their own server.

Vishal Industries

Door, cabinet & drawer handles · Gujarat

Pehle6-year-old offline desktop software
AbOne online ERP, on phone and desktop

“This manufacturing costing, I need it live.”The owner, in our first call

The big pain points
  • 100 pieces go to the job worker, 99 come back. “Ek padha hoga, dekh lenge.” Nobody kept a record of what was owed, and it added up every month.
  • Sending one challan took 5 steps: paper, desktop, PDF, find the file, WhatsApp.
  • Lost and rejected pieces never reached the cost sheet. A few pieces lost per 100 quietly made the real cost per piece about 4% higher than the cost he was quoting from.
What the new ERP does
  • Shortage ledger for every job worker. Pieces owed are tracked across challans and months, and their value is deducted from that worker's bill automatically.
  • Challan in one tap, opened in WhatsApp from his own number.
  • Live cost per piece: standard cost and true cost side by side.
Read the full case study

How it was before. Vishal Industries makes door handles, cabinet handles, drawer knobs and kitchen profiles. For six years the factory ran on a desktop program a local developer built. The owner knew every screen and was fast in it. But it worked only offline, on one computer, for one person.

His process, in his words:

Casting AavakOrdered in pieces, billed in kilos↓ Job work Javak ⇄ Aavak6–8 rounds: turning, buff, nickel, antique, gold…↓ Convert to final401 Knob = 1 Top + 1 Bottom↓ Final stockInches on the floor, millimetres for sales↓ Sales + website stock
  • Website stock typed again, separately, by hand.
  • Packing, screws and overhead had no field, so they were added “randomly”.
  • No way to see in whose process a piece went, or to whom it was given.

What the new ERP does. Built around his own terms: Aavak, Javak, challan, party.

  • Handles pieces and kilos together: stock by piece count, payment by weight.
  • Per-piece and per-inch job-work rates, so costing is right for every finish.
  • Partial receipts, multi-part assembly, and stock that can never go negative.
  • Final stock flows to the website automatically. Billing stays in his existing software.
  • Old data moved in from the desktop program. Runs online, for unlimited users.

Your doubts, answered

Owners usually ask us this.

Pehle bhi software liya tha, team ne use nahi kiya. Ab kya alag hai?

This is the most common thing we hear, and it is the first thing we design around. Software fails when the team has to change how they work to fit it. We do the opposite: screens follow your process, your Excel becomes the template, and we remove data entry instead of adding it.

Plus, our final payment comes only after 60 days of your team using it daily. If they don't use it, we don't get paid in full.

What if you can't deliver what we need?

You don't carry that risk. Each payment is released only after you accept that stage. Before signing, we can also build a working demo on one of your real orders, so you see it work on your own products first.

Have you worked in my industry?

We work with carpet, cosmetics and hardware manufacturers. Most factories share the same backbone: orders, recipe or BOM, raw material, production, wastage, packing and dispatch. What changes is your units, stages and approvals, and that is exactly what we customise.

Ready-made software is cheaper. Why custom?

Ready-made looks cheaper in year one. Then you pay per user, every year, and the bill grows with your team. And you still keep Excel on the side for whatever it doesn't handle.

With us, you pay once, own it fully, add unlimited users, and get a system that matches your process.

We already use Tally, Vyapar or other software. Do we throw it away?

No. Your billing and accounts software stays. We sync with Tally, and where you bill in Vyapar or another tool, it keeps doing that job. If another tool does one job well, we connect to it and build what sits between your systems.

3 months sounds long.

It is not 3 months of waiting. You see working screens in month 1. Your team uses it on real orders in month 2. Month 3 is full go-live.

Hamara data safe rahega? Who owns it?

You own it, fully. It runs on your server or your own cloud account. At handover we transfer source code, database and all passwords, and keep no access. Your customer list, formulas and costings never sit on someone else's platform.

Our floor staff aren't comfortable with computers.

They don't need to be. Floor screens are made for phones, as simple as WhatsApp, with big buttons and only what that person needs. We train them in person, in their language.

Kitna kharcha aayega?

It depends on your scope, so we don't guess on a page. After the call and factory visit, you get a fixed price in writing, split into the milestone plan above. No per-user fees and no yearly licence on top. This is a serious investment for a serious problem, and the proposal shows you exactly what it pays back.

What happens after go-live?

60 days of support after the final payment, then optional yearly maintenance. Since you own the code, you are free to use any developer you like.
